Peripheral adds configurable threat holograms to your HUD, helping you keep track of nearby hostile entities, including threats outside your view.

# Stuff it adds
- Threat Holograms: Displays holographic representations of nearby threats on the hud.
- All-Around Detection: Tracks threats both in front of and behind the player.
- Through-Wall Detection: Can display threats hidden behind blocks within a configurable radar range.
Listen Mode: Hold a keybind to reveal all detected enemies, regardless of whether they're in front of you or behind walls. Can be configured to require crouching.
- Compatibility: has geckolib compatibilitty.
- Very customizable.
- Menu Compatibility: Optionally display holograms over open menus, with configurable screen exclusions.
- Debug Options: Optional logging for troubleshooting and compatibility.
