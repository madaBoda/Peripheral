Peripheral adds configurable threat holograms to your HUD, helping you keep track of nearby hostile entities, including threats outside your immediate field of view.

# Stuff it adds
- Threat Holograms: Displays holographic representations of nearby threats on the hud.
- All-Around Detection: Tracks threats both in front of and behind the player.
- Through-Wall Detection: Can display threats hidden behind blocks within a configurable radar range.
- Compatibility: has geckolib compatibilitty.
- Customizable Appearance: Configure hologram colors, opacity, glow, and fade animations.
- Configurable Detection: Adjust detection range, radar range, and the maximum number of displayed targets.
- Menu Compatibility: Optionally display holograms over open menus, with configurable screen exclusions.
- Debug Options: Optional diagnostic logging for troubleshooting and compatibility.
